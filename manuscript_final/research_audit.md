## 3. Exclusion of the clustering method review

By the authors' instruction, the experiment and review titled **Clustering Method Review** is excluded from the manuscript entirely: it has no section, no finding, no comparison, no figure, no table, no sentence in the discussion or conclusion, and nothing in the manuscript is derived from it indirectly. This audit records what was excluded so that the record is complete.

The review corresponds to the clusterability investigation of the project's cluster phase: Part G of `docs/GPR_onwards_explanatory_report.md` (scripts 20, 26 and 29; `outputs/clustering_audit/`), the Stage 6 and 6b runs (`experiments/*_s6_clusterability*`, `*_s6b_skewed_null*`, `*_s6b_grm_*`, and the `pigd_measurement_audit` runs of scripts 30 to 33 that built the 6b null), the Bayesian slope-clustering analysis of script 34 (`experiments/20261015T171236Z_*`; module `src/ppmi_subtype/slope_clustering.py`), and the design notes under `experiments/_stage6b_design`. All of these are left in place, unread by the manuscript build and unreferenced by its text. The automated check in `build/verify_numbers.py` confirms that the assembled manuscript contains none of the strings "clustering method review", "clusterab", "clustering audit", "slope clustering" or "cluster phase"; the words "patient-clustered" (the bootstrap) and "within-patient clustering of Indeterminate visits" (the pre-registered O/E statistic of the Part C audit) are statistical terms unrelated to the excluded review and are retained.

Other work that touches the same modules but is not the clustering review (the Student-t variants, the Part C audits, the D-1 check and its Stage 1 diagnostics, the Stage 3 to 5 analyses) was inspected separately and included on its own merits (§2).
