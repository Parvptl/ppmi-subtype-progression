# Stage 2 M2: independent recovery test of the Rev 3 pipeline ("step 6′") — proposed plan

**Date:** 2026-10-19.

> **PROPOSED — NOT FROZEN — NO COMPUTATION AUTHORISED.**
> - It tests the procedure of doc 44 (Rev 3, proposed) on **fresh, independently generated** synthetic datasets.
> - It does not re-score Step 6, which remains **FAILED**. **D1 remains NOT REPRODUCED.**
> - Steps 7–8, calibration and real-data work remain blocked until this test is run under a frozen plan, passes, and is reviewed.

**Tags:** [P] proposed rule · [D] decision for the reviewer · [E] estimate (measured inputs where stated).

---

## 1. Purpose

To test, on data **not used** to develop the hypothesis, whether the Rev 3 pipeline recovers the 19 stage-2 parameters with calibrated intervals:
- the Rev 2 two-stage estimation with one S1-b iteration;
- then the pooled-moment re-expression S;
- with delta-method intervals from the full-chain sandwich.

The test must also show that any recovery is **genuine** and not an artefact of expressing the estimates on the truth's convention (§6).

## 3. Datasets [P]

- **R = 100** fresh datasets. The choice is justified in §4; alternatives are listed in §12.
- Each is generated by `simulate_groups` from the frozen truth on the real design with its own seed `[42, 49, 1, r]`.
- They are independent of the five development datasets (`[42, 45, 1, r]`) and of each other.
- **Regeneration check:** for r = 0 and r = R − 1, the dataset is generated twice; the two copies must be byte-identical (SHA-256 of the flattened responses).

## 4. Number of datasets and adequacy of the "≥ 4/5" rule [P]

**Operating characteristics** (exact binomial; treating the 19 components as independent, an approximation):

| rule | P(component passes \| true coverage 0.95) | P(all 19 pass \| 0.95) | P(passes \| 0.90) | P(passes \| 0.85) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| doc 16 D3: ≥ 4 of 5 | 0.977 | **0.648** | 0.919 | 0.835 |
| R = 80, ≥ 71/80 | 0.994 | 0.883 | 0.723 | 0.221 |
| **R = 100, ≥ 88/100** | 0.9985 | **0.973** | 0.802 | **0.247** |
| R = 120, ≥ 107/120 | 0.997 | 0.949 | 0.687 | 0.122 |

**The ≥ 4/5 rule is inadequate as a sole criterion.** It fails a perfectly calibrated procedure about 35 % of the time, yet passes a component with 80–85 % coverage 74–84 % of the time. It cannot detect bias of the size seen in Step 6 reliably.

**Proposed:** R = 100, with coverage and bias criteria (§5). The D3 rule is still **reported** on fresh datasets 0–4, as a historical comparator only.

## 5. Acceptance criteria [P] (all must hold)

For **each of the 19 components** of S(ψ̂), using the primary interval method:

- **C1 — coverage:** the truth lies in the 95 % interval in **≥ 88 of 100** datasets.
- **C2 — bias:** **|mean z| ≤ 0.30**, where z = (S(ψ̂)_k − ψ_true,k)/SE_S,k.
  - Per component, the false-fail probability is 0.003 if z ~ N(0, 1).
  - A true mean z of 0.5 passes with probability 0.02, and of 0.3 with probability 0.5.

For **each of the 17 relabelling-invariant functionals** (§6.2), using delta-method intervals from the same full-chain V, **without any transformation**:

- **C3 — coverage:** ≥ 88/100.
- **C4 — bias:** |mean z| ≤ 0.30.

**Not gated, reported for every component and functional:**
- SD of z, with its χ²(99) 95 % interval;
- mean error and RMSE;
- the ratio of the empirical SD of the errors to the mean SE;
- coverage under the secondary V;
- the D3 ≥ 4/5 rule on datasets 0–4.

**Familywise behaviour (approximate, under independence).** With all criteria holding at nominal, the chance of passing C1 and C2 is about 0.92. Adding C3 and C4 lowers it, but the functionals are strongly correlated with the ψ components, so the actual familywise rate is between about 0.86 and 0.92. **[D]** Accept this, or loosen C3/C4 to a guard role (§12).

## 6. Checks separating genuine recovery from a transformation artefact [P]

1. **No truth in S.**
   - The transformation function takes only ψ̂ and the design (a code-review item and a unit test on the function signature).
   - A run-time test recomputes S(ψ̂) for dataset 0 with ψ_true replaced by an arbitrary vector in the comparison code. S(ψ̂) must be bit-identical.
2. **Relabelling-invariant functionals.** These need no convention: they are identical on every relabelled scale. Their truth values come from ψ_true and the true items. Their intervals use the full-chain V on (ψ₂, φ₂), by the delta method. They are **co-primary** (C3, C4). Passing C1–C2 while failing C3–C4 would indicate apparent improvement, and the test **fails**.

   For s ∈ {T, P}, with b = (intercept_T, slope_T, intercept_P, slope_P):

   | # | functional |
   |---|---|
   | 1 | β1_s / √D_int,s |
   | 2 | γ_s / √D_int,s |
   | 3 | a_{s,1}(β0_s − τ_{s,1,1}) (item 1, first threshold) |
   | 4 | ½ log(D_slope,s / D_int,s) |
   | 5 | ½ log(Σ_ε,ss / D_int,s) |

   That is 5 × 2 = 10, plus the 6 correlations of D and the correlation of Σ_ε: **17 functionals**.
3. **Control arm (fixed true items), datasets 0–9.** Stage 2 with the generating items (the Step 4 procedure), then S. Because the true items fix the truth's scale, S should change these estimates by **≪ 1 SE**.
   - Report max_k |S(ψ̂)_k − ψ̂_k|/SE_k and the moments (μ_s, v_s) against (0, 1).
   - A large change would mean the convention manufactures differences, so the test is **reserved for review**.
4. **Raw results alongside.** The untransformed ψ̂ is reported with its own coverage, bias and z, together with the distribution of (c_s, d_s) over the datasets, so the size of the re-expression is visible.
5. **Non-binding comparator.** The N(0, 1)-only pipeline, S(ψ̂⁽¹⁾), with its fixed-item delta intervals.

## 7. Diagnostics and numerical stability [P] (reported for every dataset)

**Convergence of every fit:** status, message, iterations, evaluations, ‖∇‖ (B11's 0.934 shown for reference only), bound hits, P1/P2 penalty evaluations by reason, non-ND patients at accepted evaluations.

**At each optimum:** Hessian PD (min/max eigenvalue); the reported evaluation (fallback patients, budget triggers).

**Transformation identities (≤ 1e-8):**
- S(ψ_true) = ψ_true;
- idempotence of S(ψ̂);
- invariance of S under relabelling;
- G annihilating the relabelling directions (≤ 1e-6 relative).

**Sandwich checks:**
- per-patient score additivity (≤ 1e-8 relative);
- the relabelling identity C₂v_φ + H₂v_ψ ≈ 0 (≤ 1e-5 relative);
- FD Jacobian symmetry;
- the saved-NLL reproduction.

**FD-step sensitivity** (datasets 0–2): H₂ and C₂ at h = 2e-4 vs 1e-4. Relative SE_S differences are reported.

**Item recovery** (reported, not gated): re-expressed items against the true items (correlation, max error), and the coverage of item intervals from V_φφ.

**Le00_log secondary analysis** (doc 44 §5): first-order tremor/PIGD and affine/non-affine attribution; bias; SD of z; primary-vs-secondary-V coverage. Reported only; it never rescues a failure.

## 8. Sequence, outcome classes and stop conditions [P]

**Sequence:**
1. Freeze docs 44 and 45. Implement the driver and tests (new files only).
2. Unit tests, then a smoke code path (60 patients, *not a result*).
3. Preflight in the job: frozen locks; code hashes; panel MD5; truth equal to the record; seed list.
4. **Pipeline regression check.** The Rev 3 driver run on the **old** dataset `[42, 45, 1, 0]` must reproduce:
   - Step 6's ψ̂⁽¹⁾ and ψ̂⁽²⁾ bitwise (before S);
   - D7's S(ψ̂⁽²⁾) to ≤ 1e-12;
   - D4's full-chain SEs to ≤ 1e-6 relative.

   This confirms that only S and the interval method are new. A mismatch stops the run.
5. Fresh datasets 0–1. Then the **runtime gate**: each stage-2 fit ≤ 60 min, and the projected total ≤ the budget cap (§9). Otherwise stop.
6. Fresh datasets 2…R−1, and the control arm on datasets 0–9.
7. Criteria (§5), post-run integrity, report. **Stop for review.**

**Outcome classes** (doc 37 style):

| class | condition | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **INTEGRITY STOP** | a preflight or regression check fails | no verdict |
| **INCOMPLETE** | an execution error, a runtime-gate or budget breach, or fewer than R datasets completed | no verdict. No automatic rerun; any rerun only after review, with a defect note |
| **COMPLETE, no flag** | all R datasets completed without flagged events | formal **PASS** if C1–C4 all hold, otherwise **FAIL** |
| **COMPLETE, with flags** | flagged events (§8.1) occurred | criteria computed with the edge-case rules of doc 37 E-6 (a non-PD or singular H gives that dataset's components "not covered"; a non-finite SE gives "not covered"). For each criterion, the dependence statement is computed: does the outcome change if every flagged dataset is counted as covered vs not covered? If no criterion's outcome depends on the flagged datasets, the formal verdict stands with the flags reported. Otherwise it is **RESERVED FOR REVIEW** |

The flag rule for R = 100 is **[D]**: doc 37 reserved the verdict on any flag. With 100 datasets, the dependence rule is proposed instead.

**8.1 Flags** (per dataset):
- **F1:** a stage-2 fit not converged.
- **F2:** an item fit not converged, or a ∉ [0.05, 20].
- **F3:** a bound hit at the final estimate.
- **F4:** a fallback or budget event at the final optimum.
- **F5:** a transformation-identity failure (> 1e-8).
- **F6:** the sandwich relabelling identity > 1e-5.

If **more than 10 % of datasets are flagged**, the run is completed but classified **RESERVED FOR REVIEW** regardless.

**Hard stop conditions:**
- an integrity or regression mismatch;
- an execution error;
- a runtime-gate breach;
- the cumulative cost reaching the budget cap;
- any non-finite objective or estimate at an accepted optimum.

**No setting, tolerance or criterion may change during or after the run.**

## 11. What a pass or fail would and would not establish

**A PASS** would establish that, for the a-priori truth and the real design, the Rev 3 pipeline gives approximately calibrated 95 % intervals and small bias for all 19 components on the pooled-moment convention, and for 17 convention-free functionals. That would satisfy the binding recovery pre-requisite of the (amended) sequence, after review.

**A FAIL** would establish that Rev 3 does not meet its own pre-registered criteria. The failing components and functionals, with the Le00_log analysis, would guide any further amendment.

**Neither** would establish:
- performance under other truths, designs or misspecification;
- anything about real PPMI data, model fit, subtypes or D1.
