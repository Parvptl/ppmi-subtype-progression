**Pre-specified criteria** (both seed sets): material difference **yes** (0.0177 = 1.3 SE); reference closer to the truth **yes**; gap decreasing in σ_P **yes**. All three hold, so the result **supports** the mechanism.

**Further facts:**
- **Size and trend of the gap.** Across the whole slice, the Laplace curve sits about 53–61 log-units below the reference. That is the sum over 934 patients of the per-patient negative errors seen in doc 17, averaging about −0.06 per patient. The gap becomes more negative by about 1.0 log-unit per 0.01 increase in σ_P. That σ_P-dependent tilt is what moves the maximum.
- **Drops from each curve's maximum.**
  - At the truth: Laplace 1.91, reference 0.17 log-units.
  - At the Laplace MLE: reference 0.77.
- **Monte Carlo stability.** The two seed sets agree to 0.0002 in the argmax and to within 0.1 log-units at every point. With common random numbers, the Monte Carlo SE of each total is about 0.13.

## 4. Reading

**Observed on this dataset:**
- Replacing the Laplace approximation by the D3 reference shifts the σ_P maximum of the slice from 0.380 to 0.397: by 0.018, or 1.3 SE, toward the truth (0.405).
- The shift comes from a Laplace error that grows with σ_P.
- The reference maximum lies 0.6 SE below the truth, the Laplace maximum 1.9 SE below.

**What this supports.** The pre-specified evidence supports the proposed mechanism: stage 2's Laplace approximation biases the PIGD visit-level SD downward. On dataset 0 the reference curve accounts for about two-thirds of the gap between the Laplace estimate and the truth; the remainder (0.008) is within the sampling variation of a single dataset's estimate.

**What it does not establish** (not claimed as causation):
1. **One dataset.** The recovery bias was seen in 5 of 5 datasets (doc 18). The slice was computed for one only.
2. **A conditional slice, not a profile.** The other 18 parameters are held at the *Laplace* MLE. A full reference-likelihood maximisation could move them (for example D's PIGD components or ρ) and change where σ_P peaks. The direction and size of that effect are untested.
3. **The reference is itself an approximation for 502 patients:** importance sampling with a t₅ proposal centred on the Laplace mode. It is stable across seeds, but a poor proposal would bias all grid points in a correlated way. AGQ with 7 nodes per dimension (432 patients) was not checked against more nodes here.
4. **The quadratic vertex is a summary of a 7-point grid.** The reference curve is flat near its top (within 0.17 between 0.3925 and 0.4053), so its argmax is less sharply located than the Laplace one.
5. **The stage-1 contribution** (doc 18: β0_P bias and part of the SD bias) is outside this diagnostic. The full two-stage pipeline would still need its own repair.
